## Brand & Style

This design system embodies a calm, warm, and magical sanctuary for spoken language mastery. Tailored for ambitious Hindi-speaking learners navigating the transition to confident English fluency, the aesthetic abandons dry educational gamification in favor of deep-night tranquility and luminous empowerment. Speaking anxiety transforms into quiet confidence through a private, non-judgmental space that feels like a twilight conversation with an intuitive mentor.

The visual ethos blends **Modern Frosted Glassmorphism** with subtle **Sacred Geometric Accents**. It relies on deep, atmospheric gradients layered beneath translucent glass tiles, delicate hairline borders, and radiant glowing rings. Motifs inspired by traditional rangoli geometry appear as whisper-thin vector filaments, framing speech metrics and interactive voice waves without visual clutter. Soft 3D lighting creates tangible tactile warmth, reassuring the learner across every vocal attempt.

## Colors

The palette establishes an immersive nocturnal sanctuary, contrasting cosmic indigo depths against vivid warmth:

- **Surface & Canvas (Neutral):** Deep midnight indigo (`#0F1030`) transitions into twilight violet (`#2A1B5E`) for the primary canvas gradient. Layered glass surfaces sit at `rgba(42, 27, 94, 0.45)` with soft specular edges.
- **Primary Energy (Solar Coral & Saffron Glow):** A dual-point gradient running from radiant saffron (`#FFB347`) to living coral (`#FF6B6B`), anchored around `#FF7A59`. Used for active speaking states, primary calls-to-action, and speech-streak highlights.
- **Secondary Precision (Electric Teal):** `#2DD4BF` acts as a crystalline focus color. Reserved for pronunciation correctness, accurate phoneme feedback, real-time waveform tracking, and active voice listening nodes.
- **Tertiary Mystique (Soft Lavender):** `#B8A9FF` handles secondary actions, instructional copy accents, Hindi script transliteration guides, and subtle ambient glows.
- **Support & Feedback:** Gentle Rose (`#FF8FA3`) provides tender correction hints and gentle error states, ensuring mispronunciation alerts never feel punishing or harsh.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy orchestrates a multi-script voice experience:

- **Expressive Headlines:** Epilogue provides sculpted geometric warmth, delivering editorial character and commanding presence to prompt titles and coaching milestones.
- **User Interface & Conversational Text:** Plus Jakarta Sans provides friendly, open counters and high legibility across small mobile viewports, ensuring dialogue transcripts and prompts remain effortless to parse.
- **Devanagari & Hinglish Guides:** Noto Sans renders Hindi translations, phonetic assists, and cultural contextual hints with structural clarity, matching Plus Jakarta Sans's optical x-height.
- **Metrics, Timers & Scores:** Space Grotesk offers crisp technical contrast for fluency percentiles, pitch variance, speech pacing (words-per-minute), and vocal accuracy meters.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout operates on a flexible 4-column structure for mobile, reflowing to 8 columns on tablet and 12 columns on desktop viewports. Since the application is voice-first, screen density is kept intentionally airy to minimize cognitive overload while vocalizing.

- **Mobile Viewport (< 640px):** Employs safe-area anchored bottom sheets for prompt suggestions, placing the interactive voice orb in the lower-middle thumb zone. Margins remain generous (`1.25rem`) to isolate practice phrases.
- **Large Screens & Tablets (≥ 768px):** Adapts to a split dual-card canvas: conversational cues and Hinglish contextual guidance span the left pane, while real-time phoneme feedback and waveform metrics occupy the right.
- **Vertical Rhythm:** Spacing between prompt lines and transcript tokens adheres to multiples of `space-md` (16px), giving speaking exercises breathing room.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is conveyed through luminous glassmorphism and multi-layered glow fields rather than flat drop shadows:

- **Base Ambient Canvas:** Background surfaces use deep gradient meshes with soft ambient saffron and violet orbs blurred at 120px radius.
- **Frosted Tier 1 (Cards, Transcripts):** `background: rgba(42, 27, 94, 0.45)`, `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`, bordered by a 1px hairline stroke with a top-down linear gradient: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.18)` down to `rgba(184, 169, 255, 0.04)`.
- **Frosted Tier 2 (Flyouts, Active Word Tooltips):** `background: rgba(22, 17, 56, 0.75)`, `backdrop-filter: blur(24px)`, framed by an electric teal rim (`rgba(45, 212, 191, 0.3)`).
- **The Luminescent Aura (Voice Orb & Actions):** Active voice inputs float with ambient drop-glows: `box-shadow: 0 0 40px rgba(255, 107, 107, 0.35), 0 0 80px rgba(255, 179, 71, 0.2)`.
- **Rangoli Filigree Overlays:** Laser-thin SVG vector lines (`0.5px`, opacity 15%) layered between glass levels to inject delicate tactile heritage without competing with copy.

## Shapes

The interface balances modern geometry with friendly curvature using roundedness level `2`:
- Standard components, phoneme chips, and transcript cards leverage `0.5rem` to `1rem` corner radiuses (`rounded-lg`), softening rectangular rigidity.
- Floating voice controls, session timers, and prompt pills scale to complete circular capsules (`rounded-full`).
- Visual nodes for speech inflection and audio wave peaks are tapered smoothly to avoid aggressive sharp points.

## Components

### Glowing Voice Orb (Primary Trigger)
The heart of the application. A multi-layered interactive sphere featuring a core gradient (`#FFB347` to `#FF6B6B`), enveloped by concentric pulsing rings (`rgba(45, 212, 191, 0.3)`). Idle state exhibits a gentle breathing animation (scale 0.98 to 1.02); listening state morphs in real time based on input audio decibels, sending ripple rings outward over frosted glass panels.

### Buttons & Voice Triggers
- **Primary CTA:** Saffron-to-Coral horizontal gradient fill, white semi-bold text, inset top edge highlight (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.3)`), with an outer diffuse warm shadow.
- **Secondary Glass Buttons:** Translucent violet background with soft lavender borders, transitioning to an electric teal glow upon hover or active state.

### Interactive Phoneme & Transcript Chips
Phrases spoken by the learner appear as live frosted word chips. 
- Words pronounced accurately emit an Electric Teal under-glow with subtle check icons.
- Words needing improvement feature a soft Rose outline (`#FF8FA3`) that expands into a bilingual pronunciation coaching card upon touch.

### Cards & Feedback Panes
Frosted container panels with dynamic backdrops. Score summaries feature large Space Grotesk statistics with Rangoli-inspired geometric motifs radiating from the center ring, celebrating speaking milestones with gentle elegance.

### Bilingual Translation Drawer
Sliding bottom sheets combining English prompts with inline Hinglish transliteration pills. Tapping any word toggles instantaneous audio playback in both native Indian English accents and clear Hindi pronunciation breakdowns.
